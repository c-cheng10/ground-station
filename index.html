Data and methods

The explorer requests data from NASA POWER when a study area is selected. Each annual point averages nine grid cells from NASA’s MERRA-2, which combines observations with a weather model. A straight line fitted across the historical years gives the change per decade. The 95% interval accounts for correlation between nearby years. The verdict uses the Holm-adjusted p-value across the eight tests for the selected region.
